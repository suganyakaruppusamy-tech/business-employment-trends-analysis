## 📌 Project Overview

Business employment trends provide valuable insights into workforce growth, industry performance, and seasonal employment patterns. This project analyzes New Zealand's Business Employment dataset published by Stats NZ to understand employment behavior across industries between **2011 and 2026**.

The analysis focuses on identifying long-term employment growth, comparing workforce participation across industries, and examining quarterly employment trends through statistical analysis and visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze quarterly business employment trends from **2011–2026**
- Compare employment levels across industries
- Identify industries with long-term growth, decline, or stability
- Examine seasonal employment patterns using Actual, Seasonally Adjusted, and Trend data
- Generate actionable business insights through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

---

# 📌 Key Insights

Some major findings include:

- Business employment showed an overall upward trend across the study period.
- Health Care & Social Assistance remained one of the largest employment sectors.
- Construction experienced significant long-term growth.
- Seasonal employment variations were evident across quarters.
- Trend-adjusted data revealed more stable long-term employment movement compared to Actual values.
